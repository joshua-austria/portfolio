About Me

I'm a 4th-year Information Technology student with a passion for designing and developing websites. I enjoy combining creativity with technical skills to build user-friendly and visually engaging websites.

I'm also interested in how AI can improve the way developers work. I use AI tools to plan features, write code faster, and track down bugs, then I review and test the results myself so I understand what I'm building.

My dream is to become a Front-End Developer, but I see it as just the starting point of my career. Someday, I aspire to grow into a Full-Stack Developer, expanding my skills in both front-end and back-end development.
